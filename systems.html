My work at DOH

What I do at the Department of Health: building and maintaining systems, giving technical assistance and teaching.

I build and maintain the internal systems of the Department of Health. e-LisensiyaLaravelVueSanctumSQL Server is an e-licensing system for DOH-RO XII, with a Vue front end, a Laravel API, Sanctum for authentication and SQL Server for storage. PTSLaravel is a maternal health referral and patient tracking system for DOH CHD XII that includes postnatal care records, where most of my work is backend: schema design and security. Supply Inventory v2ASP.NET Web FormsC# tracks office supplies and runs on ASP.NET Web Forms, which I maintain and extend. The Procurement systemPHPPHPMakerTCPDF is built in PHP and turns Purchase Requests and Abstracts of Quotation into print-ready PDF forms, and I also work on a Human resource management system for the office's human resource records. Beyond building, I keep these systems running through ongoing maintenance, give technical assistance to their users, and serve as a resource speaker teaching the EMR system.
